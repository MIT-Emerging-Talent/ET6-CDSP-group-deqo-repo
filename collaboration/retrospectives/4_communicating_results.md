# Retrospective

## ✨ Team Vibe Check

This milestone was pure energy. Everyone was buzzing with ideas, experimenting with
ways to make our findings resonate beyond numbers and charts.
The team’s enthusiasm was contagious... we felt like a group
of data storytellers on a mission.

## Turning Numbers into Action

We started with a clear mission: FGM awareness and actionable advocacy.
Our target audience was broad,
NGOs, health professionals, policymakers, religious figures, community members,
but that only fueled our creativity.

We honed the core message:

- FGM kills, causes lifelong trauma, affects women and children, and
  is not required by religion.

- We asked ourselves constantly: What do we want them to learn? How do we want
  them to act? This guided every design decision.

- Creating the artifact was a game-changer.

The group came together like never before. Ideas bounced,
arguments sparked (in a good way), and we pushed each other to make our
communication both scientifically grounded and emotionally compelling.

## Continue Doing

- Collaborating dynamically... everyone's voice mattered.
- Using creativity and empathy to turn statistics into stories that move people.

## Start Doing

## Lessons Learned

- Facts plus narrative equals impact.

- Even when constraints are high (cultural resistance, security risks, internet access),
clever multi-channel strategies can still reach audiences.

- Collaboration and trust allow us to take risks in messaging that might
  have felt impossible individually.

## Strategy vs. Board

### What parts of your plan went as expected?

We identified a broad target audience, created a clear core message,
and designed a comprehensive communication artifact.

### What parts of your plan did not work out?

Some channels (like rural internet outreach) have limited reach,
requiring us to think of creative workarounds.
