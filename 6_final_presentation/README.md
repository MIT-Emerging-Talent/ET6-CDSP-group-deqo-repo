## Introduction  

We are **Group Deqo** – a team of students from MIT Emerging Talent.  
Our mission: to shed light on the hidden consequences of FGM and to propose ways
 of breaking its generational cycle through awareness, data, and advocacy.  

FGM has affected over **99% of women in Somalia**, leaving behind physical,
emotional, and social scars. With this project, we combined data, testimonies
and storytelling to call for collective action to end this harmful practice.  

---

## Problem Statement  

**Research Question:**  
How can data, storytelling, and awareness campaigns break the intergenerational
 cycle of FGM in Somalia?  

**Why it matters:**  

- ⚠️ Severe health risks: infection, complications in childbirth, lifelong pain.
- 💔 Emotional trauma: silence, stigma, and reduced quality of life.  
- 🌍 Cultural entrenchment: passed from mothers to daughters despite the dangers.

---

## Methodology  

We followed a multi-layered approach to build our project:  

- **Research:** Statistics, global health reports and testimonies on FGM.  
- **Storytelling:** Created *The Broken Cycle* comic book to tell the
    generational impact.  
- **Awareness Tool:**
  - Dashboard with statistics  
  - Scientific facts and testimonies  
  - Clear calls to action  
  - Recommendations  

---

## Results  

Our investigation highlighted:  

- FGM causes **lifelong medical and emotional complications**.  
- It persists mainly due to **cultural traditions and misinformation**.  
- Storytelling (through visuals and testimonies) is a **powerful awareness tool**
  that can change perceptions and inspire advocacy.  

---

## Conclusion  

FGM is **not a tradition to preserve** — it is a harm to end.  
By combining **data-driven insights** with **creative storytelling**, awareness
campaigns can reach communities more effectively and help break the cycle for
future generations.  

**Key recommendations:**  

- Intensify educational outreach.  
- Provide medical and emotional support for survivors.  
- Mobilize community leaders to advocate against FGM.  
- Use visual storytelling to make the issue relatable.  

---
