# Retrospective

## ✨ Team Vibe Check

This milestone stretched us thin. Everyone wanted to move fast,
but we kept circling the same question: which dataset do we actually ground
ourselves on?
It felt like being in quicksand... every time we thought we'd settled,
new questions pulled us back down.

---

## Lessons in Focus

The real challenge was breaking free from waiting.
Once we stopped holding out for the perfect dataset and decided to
build on what we had,
the fog began to clear. It was less about “what's missing” and more about
“what can we do now.”

---

## Stop Doing

- Waiting for perfect clarity before making decisions

## Continue Doing

- Being open about uncertainties and frustrations
- Finding creative ways to adapt when plans fall through
- Supporting each other through the messy decision-making

## Start Doing

- Trusting our own direction instead of relying too heavily on external input
- Setting clear “move on” deadlines when answers don't come

## Lessons Learned

- Sometimes the bravest move is to stop searching and just commit.

---

## Strategy vs. Board

### What parts of your plan went as expected?

We set up roles, explored widely, and everyone put effort into digging through
the possibilities.

### What parts of your plan did not work out?

- We assumed our partner's data would guide us, but that guidance never came.
That slowed us down more than we expected.

### Did you need to add things that weren't in your strategy?

- Yes. Independent dataset reviews and collective decision-making
became our main way forward.

### Or remove extra steps?

- Yes. We had to let go of waiting and trim back scattered dataset explorations.
